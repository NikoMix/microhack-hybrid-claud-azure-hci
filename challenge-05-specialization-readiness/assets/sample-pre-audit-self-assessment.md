## Why the B.2.x / B.3.1 verdicts are "Partial"

> *Northwind Cloud* assembled and indexed every artifact (structure complete), but the
> **amount**, **distinct-customer count**, **qualifying window**, and **required certified
> headcount** cannot be declared **Met** until verified against the **current Module B
> requirements PDF**. Marking them **Partial** — not **Met** — is the correct, honest posture.
